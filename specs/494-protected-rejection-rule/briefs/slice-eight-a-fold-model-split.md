# Coder brief, slice eight-a: the fold model moves into small modules

The whole of slice eight-a for the writer, with its terminal condition. It is the move-only checkpoint of the review slice (slice five-b) that had to wait for the fold lane's changes; those are
now on main and in this branch. The binding and the limits are in the [mandate](../mandate.md); the plan is the [economics plan](../economics-plan.md); the proof is the [gate](../gate.md), versions 4 and 5,
and its slice five-b paragraph, which applies here unchanged. Read the constitution at `.specify/memory/constitution.md` and `AGENTS.md` before you start.

## Story

As the author of slices seven-b, seven-c, eight (rejection) and ten (folds), I add declarations to small, cohesive modules: the fold model lives in modules of a few hundred lines, moved without any change of
meaning, so that nothing I add lands in a module of more than a thousand lines or has to be found among unrelated groups.

## Subject

The declarations that define or admit a fold, which five-b left in the facade files on purpose: in `lean/Singular/Model.lean` the fold's allowed edges, deltas and transition, the action deltas, the refusal,
the result, the step, the batch fold and its accumulator, the batch rule the fold lane added (a batch cannot burn what it creates), the fold's admission through every request's deadline, the exits and their
payments, the transaction builder and its fold branch; in `Lemmas.lean` and `Statements.lean` the lemmas and statements that unfold them (preservation, inversions, transaction rows, the batch laws, the
fold lane's statements). Read the five-b review (`/code/singular-protected-rejection/.orch/protected-rejection/to-494-sonnet-2/commit-owner-5c/handoffs/lean-review.md`, findings 2, 3 and 10) for the
original proposal. The tree is the one after the rebase onto main: the fold lane's declarations are in it.

## The fence

Exactly that of slice five-b, restated:

- A move with no change of meaning: no statement is weakened, no proof changes but its place, every existing control stays green, the `AXIOMS` line and digest of every statement are unchanged, and the
  qualified names of every declaration are unchanged (the namespaces stay; a move that would change a qualified name is listed, not applied).
- The corpora of scenarios, the transaction baseline and the driver protocol version stay byte-identical; only the generated provenance envelopes and the records that quote a declaration by path change.
- No rename. No new declaration. Facades stay, so every existing import path works.
- Every consumer of a moved declaration is updated in the same checkpoint by a repository-wide search for the old path: the tools that read the model as a module set (taught in slice five-b), the coverage
  inventory and tests, the conformance code that quotes a path, the simulator mirror, the documents. Spec pages are mine: list each reference you find.
- The fold lane's declarations arrive in their new homes with the text they have on main, byte for byte (compare with `origin/main`).
- Generated files are regenerated by their commands, never hand-edited.

## The shape, bounded

Four checkpoints at most, in this order; about three hours in all.

1. **The move list.** Write `handoffs/move-list.md` and edit nothing: every declaration or group to move, from where to which module (names, a few hundred lines each), the facade each file keeps, the reason in one
   line, and any declaration you would not move and why. Then wait on your channels for my note.
2. **The model and the lemmas.** The fold model's definitions and the lemmas split into modules; the tools follow.
3. **The statements.** The fold statements split by the property each group proves; the tools follow.
4. **The final head.** Regeneration, the final rows and `nix develop --quiet -c just ci` once.

At every checkpoint after the first: commit, write `handoffs/review-NNN.md` (the commit; the move list with the evidence that each move is meaning-preserving: the text moved is identical, shown by a mechanical
comparison; the gate commands and exits; what you did not cover), journal `REVIEW-REQUESTED review=NNN sha=<sha>`, and continue only when it is approved or I say so.

## Gate

The "always" rows of the [gate](../gate.md), version 4, at checkpoints 2 and 3; once on the final head: lint, documents including speech and narration of every edited page, code inventory, strict coverage report,
browser check and `nix develop --quiet -c just ci` once without a time limit shorter than the aggregate. One local execution of a slow carrier per checkpoint is the gate; never a retry loop.

## Terminal condition

Stop at the first of: every gate command exits as the gate states on a clean committed head (`PROOF-COMPLETE` with the moves, their evidence and the receipts); a question you cannot decide from this brief (file it as
`questions/Q-NNN-<slug>.md` with the user story of each option and journal `BLOCKED`); capacity (`COMPLETE capacity-limit` with a handoff that makes the remainder mechanical). Do not touch anything listed under the
files this ticket must not touch.
