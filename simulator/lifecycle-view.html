Boundaries you can see

No death oracle. The quorum route uses an explicit threshold of configured signer key hashes. It infers nothing about death, incapacity, time, or identity, and it does not require the controller signer.

Raw-address payment limit. Retiring a name prevents name-based resolution, but it cannot prevent someone from sending directly to a previously saved raw Cardano address. The model preserves canonical binary base or enterprise addresses as routing data; it does not construct a transaction, prove wallet ownership, execute a payment, or inspect a ledger.

Claim cancellation is separate. The m1-naming profile can withdraw an unfulfilled claim with separate cancellation approval. The request stores its refund address when queued, and withdrawal must copy it; redirect, Insert-certification-only, post-fold, and replay attempts are refused. This does not weaken the required refusal against withdrawing a retirement request, and it invents no fee, deposit, price, bond, or refund-value schedule.
